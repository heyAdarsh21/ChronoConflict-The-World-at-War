How It All Began

WW2 didn’t start overnight. From the scars of WW1, the rise of fascism, and failed diplomacy, the world was pushed into chaos. It began with a single invasion and ended with nuclear fire.

The Political Landscape

Fascism

Mussolini's Italy set the tone for authoritarian nationalism and expansionist ambition.

Nazism

Hitler’s Germany pushed Aryan supremacy and anti‑Semitism to horrifying extremes.

Communism

Stalin’s USSR remained authoritarian but became a vital part of the Allied victory.

Democracy

UK, US, and others stood against authoritarianism, despite initial hesitation.

Key Battles

  • Stalingrad: Turning point in the Eastern Front
  • D‑Day: Allied storming of Normandy
  • Midway: U.S. halts Japanese advance in the Pacific
  • Battle of Britain: Britain’s skies held against the Luftwaffe

War By the Numbers

Total casualties: 70–85 million
Military deaths: ~25 million
Civilian deaths: ~50 million
